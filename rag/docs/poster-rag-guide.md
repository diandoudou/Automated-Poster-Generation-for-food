## 1. Layout Orientation & Format

### Vertical (Portrait) Layout - 竖版
**Characteristics:**
- Taller than wide, recommended 9:16 or 3:4 aspect ratio
- Natural reading flow from top to bottom
- Optimal for mobile viewing and wall-mounted displays
- Better for stacking information vertically
- Eye-level concentration in central area

**Aesthetic Effects:**
- Elegant and formal appearance
- Emphasizes height and verticality
- Creates sense of strength and uplift
- Suitable for focal point in center
- Draws viewer's eye from top to bottom in natural reading order

**Common Applications:**
- Event posters, concert announcements
- Product promotions for single items
- Movie/film promotion posters
- Social media poster content (Instagram)
- Door/wall-mounted advertisements

**Design Considerations:**
- Place title/headline at top
- Main visual element in center
- Contact information and CTA at bottom
- Maintain 40% white space, 30% graphics, 30% text ratio
- Use vertical columns for information organization

---

### Horizontal (Landscape) Layout - 横版
**Characteristics:**
- Wider than tall, recommended 16:9 or 4:3 aspect ratio
- Multiple focal points across width
- Better for eye-level viewing range (2-4 meters)
- Accommodates dual-column or multi-section content
- More breathing room horizontally

**Aesthetic Effects:**
- Modern and dynamic appearance
- Suggests movement and flow across space
- Conveys stability and calm
- Allows for comparative layouts (before/after)
- Creates sense of openness

**Common Applications:**
- Business and corporate presentations
- Information-heavy posters (conferences, schedules)
- Landscape scenery promotions
- Split/comparison designs
- Digital display screens

**Design Considerations:**
- Distribute visual weight across width
- Use asymmetrical balance for interest
- Sectioning with subtle dividers
- Larger percentage of poster at eye-level
- Suitable for left-to-right reading flow

## 2. Color Palettes & Psychological Effects

### Warm Color Schemes (暖色系)

- **Psychological Effect:** Energy, warmth, excitement, urgency, appetite stimulation
- **Best For:** Food, events, promotions, celebrations

  - Medium saturation: Balanced, approachable, friendly
  - Low saturation: Soft, nostalgic, sophisticated
- **Common Combinations:**
  - Red + Gold: Premium, festive, traditional

  - Burnt Orange + Brown: Warm, earthy, organic

**Emotional Resonance:**
- Red: Passion, danger, confidence, excitement
- Orange: Joy, warmth, enthusiasm, friendliness
- Yellow: Happiness, optimism, caution, energy

### Cool Color Schemes (冷色系)
- **Primary Colors:** Blue, Green, Purple, Teal
- **Psychological Effect:** Calm, trust, stability, peace, nature
- **Best For:** Technology, wellness, environmental, professional
- **Saturation Levels:**
  - High saturation: Fresh, vibrant, modern
  - Medium saturation: Balanced, professional, reliable
  - Low saturation: Serene, sophisticated, luxury

**Emotional Resonance:**
- Blue: Trust, stability, serenity, sadness (context-dependent)
- Green: Growth, health, nature, harmony, money

- Teal: Tranquility, sophistication, balance

### Neutral & Mixed Schemes (中性色系)
- **Primary Colors:** Black, White, Gray, Beige, Brown
- **Usage:** Background foundation, text contrast, sophistication
- **Enhancement:** Use accent colors (10-20% of palette) for visual interest
- **Aesthetic Effect:** Minimalist, timeless, professional, elegant

**Common Neutral Combinations:**
- Black + White: High contrast, bold, dramatic
- Beige + Brown: Warm, earthy, organic, premium
- Gray + White: Modern, clean, corporate, minimal
- Black + Gold: Luxury, exclusive, premium

### Color Temperature & Mood
- **Warm Temperature (暖):** 0°C to 50°C → Happiness, energy, comfort
- **Cool Temperature (冷):** 180°C to 270°C → Calm, trust, relaxation
- **Mixed Temperature (混合):** Balanced between both → Harmony, sophistication

## 3. Typography & Font Styles

### Serif Fonts (衬线字体)
- **Characteristics:** Decorative lines at end of letters
- **Emotional Tone:** Traditional, elegant, formal, literary
- **Best For:** Premium products, cultural content, historical themes
- **Examples:** Times New Roman, Georgia, Garamond
- **Poster Usage:** Headlines, titles, body text in formal designs

### Sans-Serif Fonts (无衬线字体)
- **Characteristics:** Clean lines without decorative elements
- **Emotional Tone:** Modern, minimal, professional, tech-focused
- **Best For:** Contemporary designs, business, clean aesthetics
- **Examples:** Helvetica, Arial, Open Sans, Montserrat
- **Poster Usage:** Headlines, body text, clean layouts

### Display/Decorative Fonts (装饰字体)
- **Characteristics:** Expressive, artistic, stylized letterforms
- **Emotional Tone:** Playful, artistic, cultural, creative
- **Best For:** Event posters, artistic promotions, entertainment
- **Examples:** Script fonts, hand-drawn fonts, artistic typefaces
- **Poster Usage:** Headlines, accents, limited use (avoid overuse)

### Typography Hierarchy Strategy
1. **Headline Font Size:** 48pt+ for readability from 2+ meters
2. **Subheading Font Size:** 28-36pt
3. **Body Text Font Size:** 24-28pt minimum
4. **Maximum Fonts Per Poster:** 2-3 typefaces
5. **Font Pairing:** Bold headline + Simple body text for contrast

**Font Combination Best Practices:**
- Serif headline + Sans-serif body
- Display headline + Simple body
- Traditional serif + Modern sans-serif
- Avoid combining similar font weights or styles

## 4. Composition & Layout Structures

### Rule of Thirds (三分法)
- **Grid System:** Divide poster into 3×3 grid
- **Focal Point Placement:** Position main elements at grid intersections
- **Visual Balance:** Creates natural eye movement and tension
- **Application:** Most versatile composition rule for posters
- **Effect:** More dynamic than centered composition while maintaining balance

### Centered/Symmetric Composition (中心/对称构图)
- **Characteristics:** Main element at center, symmetrical arrangement
- **Visual Effect:** Formal, stable, calm, orderly
- **Best For:** Minimalist designs, professional content, classical aesthetics
- **Psychological Effect:** Evokes trust, reliability, formality
- **Risk:** Can appear static or boring if overused without variation

### Diagonal Composition (对角线构图)
- **Characteristics:** Elements arranged along diagonal lines
- **Visual Effect:** Dynamic, energetic, movement-oriented
- **Best For:** Action-oriented designs, modern posters, creative concepts
- **Psychological Effect:** Tension, motion, excitement, direction
- **Application:** Leading lines guide viewer's eye across poster

### Asymmetrical/Dynamic Composition (非对称构图)
- **Characteristics:** Unbalanced placement but overall visual equilibrium
- **Visual Effect:** Modern, creative, engaging, contemporary
- **Best For:** Contemporary designs, split content layouts, comparative designs
- **Psychological Effect:** Interest, surprise, creativity, dynamism
- **Technique:** Balance visual weight using color, size, and placement

### Column-Based Layout (栏位构图)
- **Single Column:** Information stacked vertically, simple flow, vertical posters
- **Two Columns:** Comparison, dual-focus, information-heavy content
- **Multi-Column:** Complex information, sections, grids
- **Hybrid:** Mix column types for varied content emphasis
- **Benefit:** Guides viewer's eye logically through content

## 6. Visual Balance & Composition Techniques

### Symmetrical Balance (对称平衡)
- **Definition:** Mirror-like arrangement of elements on both sides
- **Emotional Effect:** Formal, stable, calm, organized
- **Advantages:** Easy to understand, peaceful, professional
- **Disadvantages:** Can appear static, predictable, boring
- **Best For:** Formal events, professional services, ceremonial posters

### Asymmetrical Balance (非对称平衡)
- **Definition:** Different elements balanced through visual weight, not position
- **Techniques:**
  - Large element + multiple small elements
  - Dark color + bright color
  - Detailed area + simple area
- **Emotional Effect:** Dynamic, interesting, modern, creative
- **Advantages:** More engaging, modern, less predictable
- **Disadvantages:** Requires careful execution to avoid looking chaotic
- **Best For:** Contemporary designs, creative industries, trendy products

### Radial Balance (放射状平衡)
- **Definition:** Elements radiating outward from central point
- **Effect:** Draws attention to center, creates focal point, sense of completeness
- **Best For:** Circular designs, concentric patterns, focal point emphasis
- **Application:** Center element with supporting elements around it

### Visual Weight Distribution (视觉权重分布)
**Heavy Elements (attract more attention):**
- Large size
- Dark colors
- Bold typography
- High contrast
- Detailed areas
- Saturated colors

**Light Elements (distribute balance):**
- Small size
- Light colors
- Simple typography
- Low contrast
- Minimal details
- Desaturated colors

**Technique:** Place heavy elements carefully, balance with multiple light elements or white space

## 7. Negative Space & White Space Usage (负空间/留白)

**Importance:**
- Prevents overcrowding and visual fatigue
- Guides viewer's eye to focal point
- Creates breathing room and elegance
- Improves readability and comprehension
- Increases perceived sophistication

**Recommended Ratios:**
- Minimalist design: 50-60% white space
- Balanced design: 35-45% white space
- Content-heavy design: 20-30% white space
- Poster standard: 40% white space, 30% graphics, 30% text

**Application Strategies:**
- Use around focal point to emphasize importance
- Separate sections using white space instead of borders
- Create rhythm through spacing between elements
- Balance information density with breathing room

**Aesthetic Effects:**
- Sophistication and refinement
- Professional appearance
- Clarity and focus
- Luxury and premium feel
- Reduced visual overwhelm

## 9. Focal Point & Visual Hierarchy

### Creating Strong Focal Point (视觉焦点)
**Techniques:**
1. **Size:** Make focal point significantly larger than other elements
2. **Color:** Use contrasting color to make it stand out
3. **Contrast:** High contrast between focal point and background
4. **Placement:** Strategic positioning (center, rule of thirds intersection)
5. **Detail:** Add intricate details to draw focus
6. **Isolation:** Surround with white space

### Visual Hierarchy (视觉层级)
**Information Organization:**
1. **Primary:** Most important information (largest, boldest, most contrasting)
2. **Secondary:** Supporting information (medium size, slightly less emphasis)
3. **Tertiary:** Additional details (smallest, least emphasized)

**Implementation:**
- Headline (Primary): 48pt+, bold, high contrast
- Subheading (Secondary): 28-36pt, semi-bold
- Body Text (Tertiary): 24-28pt, regular weight
- Supporting Info (Tertiary): 18-24pt, smaller importance

**Visual Flow:**
- Eye enters at focal point
- Flows to secondary information
- Rests on tertiary details
- Natural reading path guides viewer through content

## Query Expansion Examples for RAG System

### Example 1: Input "古风"(Ancient/Traditional Chinese Style)
**Expanded Context:**
The design should incorporate traditional Chinese aesthetics with flowing lines, cloud motifs, and nature elements. Utilize classical color combinations of red, gold, black, and jade green. Typography should feature calligraphic elements or seal script fonts. Include decorative patterns such as meander borders or botanical designs (plum blossoms, bamboo). The composition often follows symmetrical balance with emphasis on elegance and refinement. Photography style may include silk textures, traditional props, and cultural artifacts. Emotional effect: elegant, cultural, nostalgic, spiritual.

### Example 2: Input "食物" (Food)
**Expanded Context:**
Food posters require appetizing visual presentation with professional food photography as the hero element. Utilize warm colors (red, orange, yellow) to stimulate appetite. Styling should include complementary props (plates, utensils, ingredients) in a balanced composition. Lighting should be warm and professional, either natural golden-hour style or controlled studio setup. High contrast makes food pop against background. Typography should be clean and readable, clearly communicating dish name, description, price, and call-to-action. Emotional effect: indulgent, appetizing, quality, comfort.

### Example 3: Input "竖版" (Vertical Format)
**Expanded Context:**
Vertical layout (9:16 or 3:4 aspect ratio) should emphasize top-to-bottom reading flow with title at top, focal point in center, and contact information at bottom. This format works well for mobile viewing and wall-mounted displays. Maintain 40% white space with balanced visual hierarchy. Central focal point should draw attention, with secondary elements supporting the main message. Asymmetrical or diagonal composition can add dynamism while maintaining vertical emphasis. Typography should be sized for readability from a distance (minimum 24pt for body text). Common applications: event posters, product promotion, social media content.

### Example 4: Input "极简" (Minimalist)
**Expanded Context:**
Minimalist design prioritizes essentials with 50-60% white space, 2-3 color maximum, and clean lines. Only include necessary elements that serve the core message. Utilize high contrast between elements for clarity. Typography should be simple, legible sans-serif fonts. Single strong focal point organizes viewer's attention. Layout should be organized and systematic. Emotional effect: sophisticated, professional, modern, calm. This approach conveys luxury and refinement through restraint rather than abundance. Remove any decorative elements that don't serve communication purpose.
